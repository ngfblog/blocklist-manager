IP lists block traffic from known malicious IP addresses at the firewall level. pfBlockerNG loads these into PF tables in RAM and blocks matching packets before they reach your network.

What are IP blocklists?Lists of known malicious IPs. pfBlockerNG downloads and blocks any packet from a listed IP before it enters your network. Best for scanners, botnets, and known attackers.

DNSBL lists block malicious domains at DNS level. When a device resolves a blocked domain, Unbound returns nothing — the connection never happens. Works even for encrypted traffic.
Active DNSBL lists
?
What are DNSBL lists?Domain blocklists fed into Unbound. When any device asks for a blocked domain, the DNS query returns nothing — blocking ads, trackers, and malware network-wide.
